## What I'm Building

**[Kōlea](https://kolea.pro)** is a newsletter and sales platform for people who write and sell what they make. You write once, it goes to your list and onto your own site, and every sale gets traced back to the email that made it. It has a slop meter that catches machine-sounding writing before your readers do, and no AI is involved in that part. It's the best software I've ever made.

**[Big Machine](https://bigmachine.io)** is where my books and courses live, along with [the newsletter](https://a.bigmachine.io). It's also where I do AI adoption consulting: helping a team skill up so they build better things, not only faster ones.

## Greatest Hits
